Step T1 Intake normalisation
As-Is Manual Baseline (SOP S1)Retyped Paper Notes
Call-centre agents retype caller details from paper notes into the claims core. Average handling time is 22 minutes and 14% of records contain data-entry errors.
Target To-Be ExecutionAgent-Structured Claim Event
The intake agent structures the call into a claim event record and the human agent only confirms the summary. Target is 9 minutes handling time and under 2% entry errors.

Step T4 Estimate reconciliation
As-Is Manual Baseline (SOP S4)Estimates by Fax and Email

Slide 2.1 Fraud Pre-Scoring on Every Claim
Step T3 Fraud pre-score
As-Is Manual Baseline (SOP S3)Email Referrals to SIU

Slide 2.2 Settlement Authority Routing
Step T5 Authority routing
As-Is Manual Baseline (SOP S5)Spreadsheet Authority Limits
Authority limits live in a spreadsheet and claims above limit are routed by email, giving a 6.2 day average settlement cycle.
Target To-Be ExecutionRule-Based Routing to the Right Approver
The authority agent routes each settlement by amount: up to 5,000 to the adjuster, up to 25,000 to the team lead, above that to the claims manager. Target cycle is 2.5 days.
